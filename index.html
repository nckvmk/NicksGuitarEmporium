Welcome To Our Guitar Shop

Welcome to our vintage guitar and amplifier shop, based in Elefsina, Greece. We specialize in the trade of carefully selected instruments and amplifiers manufactured no later than 1980, serving musicians, collectors, and enthusiasts who value quality, authenticity, and history. Every piece that enters our inventory undergoes a thorough inspection and evaluation process, ensuring transparent pricing based on originality, condition, and market value. Our mission is to bring professionalism and trust to Greece's vintage gear market while preserving the legacy of these remarkable instruments. We handle every guitar and amplifier with the utmost care and respect, committed to finding each one a new and appreciative home. Every musician or guitar afficionado is welcome to our store to give our inventory a go!

If you are interested in trading with us, please head over to the Contact page to make your inquiry. Please do note that currently we are only processing transactions either on our physical store or through our Reverb storefront. Thank you and long live rock 'n' roll!
